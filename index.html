Our Story

Born from a love of the wild

Yofox African Adventures was founded in Arusha — the gateway to Tanzania's greatest wilderness — by guides who grew up alongside these incredible landscapes. We believe every safari should be as unique as the person taking it.

With our expert team on the ground in Tanzania and a dedicated representative in the UK, we make world-class African adventures accessible to clients from every corner of the globe. Every itinerary is lovingly crafted around your dreams, pace, and budget.

Yohana Mollel

As founder of Yofox African Adventures, his passion for wildlife, deep knowledge of every park and track, and infectious warmth have made him one of the most sought-after guides in the region.
